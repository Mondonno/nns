## About the Conference

We are pleased to invite you to participate in the 30th International Conference IVUS 2025. Conference is planned to be organized in-person and online.

The conference is organized by: Vytautas Magnus University Faculty of Informatics.

With the aim to stimulate research and cooperation among the early-stage researchers, a traditional conference IVUS 2025 is the continuation of the annual conference series organized in succession by three universities: Vytautas Magnus University, Kaunas University of Technology and Vilnius University Kaunas Faculty.

The event was started in 1995. In 2017, the conference became an international event. For the reason of a long history of this conference, we left conference acronym according to its Lithuanian name “Informacinė visuomenė ir universitetinės studijos” – IVUS. And since 2017 it is a joint event that brings together our colleagues and organizing partners Silesian University of Technology, Gliwice, Poland.

These conferences are aimed at Master & PhD students together with their supervisors as well as at young scientists, researchers, practitioners of the field to share ideas, present research results and provide new scientific opportunities.

All papers will be peer-reviewed by the International Program Committee, judged with respect to their quality, originality, and relevance to session topics.

Conference post-proceedings shall be submitted to CEUR-WS.org for online publication.

## Additional information

Fees and Attendance

The attendance of conference is free. Local authors will deliver  
presentations in a traditional form. The remote presenters can present their papers using the teleconferencing system (webinar).

Conference language

The official language of the conference is English.

Registration

Registration will be available before the event.

The names and email addresses entered in this conference site will be used exclusively for the stated purposes of this conference and will not be made available for any other purpose or to any other party.

Conference location

Conference will take place in Kaunas region, Lithuania.
